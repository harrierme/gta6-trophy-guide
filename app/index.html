    Roadmap to the platinum

    Around 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies. Difficulty 4 out of 10, per PowerPyx. The story takes 60 to 80 hours, the 100% checklist about 100, the questlines 10 to 20, and the secrets and cleanup a few more. Keep cheats off the whole run: they switch off trophies and saving.

    Missables

    One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude

    It asks you to meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida. The first spot is the problem: in Chapter 1, the morning after Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony at Jason's house and accept her meditation. That moment is gone after Chapter 1, and it is not confirmed whether the trophy needs it. Do not skip it.

    Meditating with Jason and Lucia together counts twice. The last spot needs both characters, which is possible after Coffee Break in Chapter 2. Track your count under Progress, then Collections, then Exploration, then Meditations.

    Watch list: looks missable, probably is not

    These are the things players worry about missing. None of them is a confirmed missable, but one is worth doing the moment it appears.

    • Strangers & Freaks. One report says the time-sensitive Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 might cost you a strand if skipped. That is unconfirmed. Do the race the moment it appears, and the question goes away.
    • Everything after the story. Free roam continues after the credits, the 100% checklist stays open, and every questline and activity is still there. No trophy needs a mission replay, because there is none.
    • Optional missions. Some become unavailable during certain story sections, but there are more optional tasks than the 100 the checklist asks for, so missing a few does not block A Long Road.
    • The Irredeemable criminal profile. It is permanent and makes police more aggressive, but it does not block any trophy. Still, avoid pushing towards it.

    Unconfirmed triggers are not missables

    Four trophies have no confirmed trigger in any published guide yet: In The Beginning, Capital Offense, Bad Luck I Guess, and The Truth Is Out There. That means nobody has published a working method, not that they can be missed. They say so plainly in their guides.
